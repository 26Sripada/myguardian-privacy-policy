My Guardian Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep 1, 2025

Welcome to My Guardian! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use our mobile application (“App”). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide our services effectively:

a) Personal Information

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) for identity verification
  • Profile details for volunteers, NGOs, nutritionists, and ambulance drivers

b) Location Information

  • GPS location for SOS, emergency alerts, complaint tracking, and Swachh Bharat initiatives
  • Location is only used for safety and service purposes

c) Health and Activity Data

  • Step count, BMI, and other health metrics (optional, if you use health features)

d) Media Data

  • Photos, videos, or audio submitted via complaints, Swachh Bharat reporting, or other app features

e) Device and Usage Information

  • Device ID, IP address, crash reports, and app usage analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide emergency services and support (SOS, ambulance, fire/flood alerts)
  • Track complaints and Swachh Bharat activities
  • Manage volunteers, NGOs, and other service providers
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Process subscriptions, payments, and rewards
  • Send important safety notifications

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We may share information in limited cases:

  • With volunteers, NGOs, ambulance drivers, and service providers to provide the requested support
  • With government authorities if legally required
  • With trusted third-party analytics providers (in anonymized form)

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption for sensitive information stored in Firebase
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security monitoring and audits

Note: No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we strive to protect your data.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Consent withdrawal: Opt-out of location tracking or other optional features

To exercise your rights, contact us at: myguardian2601@gmail.com

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
