My Guardian Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep 1, 2025

Welcome to My Guardian! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use our mobile application My Guardian. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide our services effectively:

a) Personal Information

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Date of birth, occupation, blood group, address, state, city, pincode
  • Profile details for volunteers, ambulance drivers, business owners, and service providers
  • Profile photo (optional)

b) Emergency Contacts

  • Name, relation, and phone number of up to 3 emergency contacts (provided by you)

c) Location Information

  • GPS location for SOS alerts, complaints, Swachh Bharat reporting, and nearby business listings
  • Location is collected only with your consent and used for safety and service purposes

d) Health and Activity Data (Optional)

  • Height, weight, age, exercise details (for BMI calculator)
  • Step count and activity data (for pedometer and rewards)
  • These features are optional and collected only if you enable them

e) Media Data

  • Photos, videos, or audio submitted via complaints, Swachh Bharat initiatives, or business listings
  • For complaints submitted anonymously, your name is still stored in the backend solely for responding to you, but it is never publicly revealed

f) Device and Usage Information

  • Device ID, IP address, crash logs, performance data
  • Firebase analytics for improving app functionality

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide SOS and emergency services
  • Track complaints and Swachh Bharat initiatives
  • For anonymous complaints, your name is stored in the backend only to provide updates or revert the status to you; it is not revealed to anyone else
  • Manage volunteers, NGOs, and service providers
  • Improve app functionality, security, and user experience
  • Process business listing fees, subscriptions, and rewards
  • Send important notifications and alerts
  • Provide health and wellness features, including nutritionist consultation and BMI tracking

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With your chosen emergency contacts for SOS alerts
  • With verified volunteers, ambulance drivers, and service providers to deliver requested services
  • With certified nutritionists/dietitians if you use wellness services
  • With government authorities when legally required
  • With trusted third-party analytics and payment providers (in anonymized form)

4. Data Security

We implement strict measures to safeguard your information:

  • Data stored securely in Firebase with encryption
  • Access protected through authentication and role-based permissions
  • Regular monitoring for security and misuse

Note: No system is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your data.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask to update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Permanently delete your account and associated data
  • Consent Withdrawal: Disable location tracking or optional features at any time

To exercise your rights, contact us at: myguardian2601@gmail.com

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Permissions We Request

  • Location: For SOS alerts, complaints, Swachh Bharat, and business listings
  • Contacts & Call/SMS Access: To notify your emergency contacts
  • Storage/Camera: To upload media for complaints, Swachh Bharat, and listings
  • Activity Recognition: To track steps for wellness rewards
  • Internet: To connect to Firebase, payments, and notifications

8. Payments and Subscriptions

  • Business listing fees are securely processed via third-party payment providers (e.g., Razorpay)
  • We do not store your payment card details

9. Cookies and Analytics

  • We use Firebase Analytics and similar tools to understand app usage
  • These tools collect device and usage information in anonymized form
  • No third-party advertising cookies are used

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified via the App or email. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
